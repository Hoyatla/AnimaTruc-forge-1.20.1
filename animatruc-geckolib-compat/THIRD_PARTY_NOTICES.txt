AnimaTruc GeckoLib Bridge - Third-Party Notices

This module is an addon bridge between AnimaTruc and the official GeckoLib mod.
It does not bundle, shade, relocate, or republish GeckoLib classes.

Referenced upstream project:
- GeckoLib
- Source: https://github.com/bernie-g/geckolib
- License: MIT

Official GeckoLib remains a required external runtime dependency.
